### KEY INSIGHTS
- The average wait time for patient was approximately 35.3 min, which have also reflected on the Patient Satisfaction rate, which is an average of 4.99 out of 10
-  The busiest days were Saturday (1377 patients), Thrusday(1332), , and sunday (1318). The busiest hours were 1am, 7am and 11pm   indication need to ample staff during this period 
- The percentage of patients seen less that 30 mins waiting time is down by 40.68%, as against those who waited longer which is about 59.32%
- The highest number of patients by age grade are between 30-39, with White leading the highest by Race
- Saturday tend to be the most busiest, followed by thrusday.

### CONCULSION / RECOMMENDATION

The dataset reveals high patient volumes, moderate satisfaction levels, and common referral to general practice and orthopaedics. Monday and late night to early mornings hours are particularly busy, indication need to ample staff during this period to ensure that patient flow is smooth and safe. This will reduce the longer wait time and will increase patient satisfaction. These insight can help optimize resource allocation and improve patient care in the emergency room.
